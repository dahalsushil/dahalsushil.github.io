Research

My research integrated genomic, molecular, and bioinformatic approaches to investigate species delimitation, historical biogeography, and diversification in the genus Symphyotrichum. I employed phylogenomic, population genomic, and comparative evolutionary methods to reconstruct species relationships and infer demographic history in shaping lineage diversification. Building on this work, I expanded my research to examine morphological evolution and the ecological drivers of plant–pollinator interactions by integrating morphometric analyses, multivariate statistics, and floral chemotaxonomy, linking genomic and phenotypic variation to ecological processes.
